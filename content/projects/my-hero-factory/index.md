## Description

The game throws you in the role of the item shop keeper in a not so fantasy/medieval world. You role is to gear up the local peasants for them they become warriors (provided a little money), and die happily for the sake of the kingdom (and your shop of course) facing hordes of organised monsters.

## Control Scheme

Equip the hero: space
