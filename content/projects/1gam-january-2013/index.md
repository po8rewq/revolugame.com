## An idea

My first game will be a <strong>2D platformer game</strong> (probably only a flash version). 

The character will be controlled with the arrow keys, and the mouse (for the shooting part). 

The world is <strong>made of cubes</strong>, and some parts can be <strong>collected</strong> with your special weapon (actually I think of a <strong>modified vacuum cleaner</strong>) and <strong>dropped off</strong> to access some place not reachable. The world will be <strong>modifiable</strong> but only at some point. The player will be able to collect only special cubes, and drop them off on special places (it's closer to a <strong>puzzle game</strong> than a sandbox game). The player can only move one block at the same time, and can't use a weapon during this time.

I intend to include different types of monsters (that have basic movements, can shoot, fly...) and probably NPC for the story part (and why not optional quest). 

For the moment (and I think it's going to stay) I will use the <a href="http://www.lostgarden.com/" title="LostGarden">lostgarden</a>'s Planet Cute PNG. It's been too long since I wanted to use it in one of my games ^^.
