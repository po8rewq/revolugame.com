I have integrated a **tutorial** which is playable as a little party of **Coloring**.

## How is it working?

Each turn you play, you have to complete a **small challenge**, receiving explanations on how to:
 
 * place a card on the board
 * merge cards
 * understand the UI
 * unblock a color
 * reach a score
